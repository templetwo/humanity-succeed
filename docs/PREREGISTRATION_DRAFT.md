# Preregistration — fill and approve before execution

**Status:** unregistered draft. This file is not a public preregistration or authority to train.

## Registration identity

Study name; study ID; pilot or confirmatory; owner; dated protocol version and SHA-256; actual registry receipt if published; author/reviewer/custodian roles; exact model/data/code/runtime identities; declared prior exposures.

## Motivation and claim scope

Use BUILD_SPEC §1. Distinguish narrow safety-response generalization from sustained action-level conduct. State that this dataset reflects the named contributors' reviewed commitments, not all human values. List affirmative-help outcomes explicitly. Do not infer felt desire or AGI behavior.

## Hypotheses, estimands and populations

Declare H1 and H2 from PROTOCOL §3, the target scenario distribution, fixed versus random checkpoint scope, family weights, unit of independence, train/dev/pilot/confirmatory root separation, allowed acceptable outcomes and primary scoring modes. Declare secondary/exploratory analyses in advance.

## Intervention and control

Exact frozen base checkpoint and tokenizer; procedural and curriculum dataset manifests; matched-training audit; intentionally differing content; residual semantic differences; prompt/principles hash and authorship exposure; optional ICL selection; optimizer/update/checkpoint rules; seeds and generation settings. Confirm no fabricated or harmful control targets were introduced merely for matching.

## Cases and custody

Root graph and provenance; licenses/permissions; actual independent reviews; custodian and holdout access policy; all prior exposures; duplicate/leak lint report; prompt/rubric phrase flags with resolutions. Freeze train/dev material separately from confirmatory custody.

## Commissioning evidence

Actual evaluator commit; constructed suite identity; 120 development / 40 heldback membership by group; failures and replacement history; semantic human-review status; exact discrimination results. No population error-rate certificate from constructed fixtures.

## Workload and bounds

Exact cells, trained replicates, unique base samples, scenario roots, variants, episode count, model-call ceiling, output/context-token limits, timeout/memory/disk policies, training update counts, human review count and optional ICL workload. Attach the machine-generated plan and its hash.

## Statistical settings — all require explicit values

Primary comparisons; practical-gain/equivalence/NI margins and justification; missingness trigger; family weights; interval method and multiplicity; estimator qualification receipts; bootstrap seeds/draws; sample-size simulation assumptions; seed-scope limits; semantic-review aggregation rule; worst-case missingness sensitivity; all classification inequalities.

Candidate margins in configs are proposals. Explain changes rather than quietly keeping a default. Do not claim a smaller change is worthless merely because it is harder to detect.

## Stopping, deviations, and retries

Predeclared invalidity and capability triggers; interruption procedure; allowed retry rules and attempt counting; no effect peeking; who can approve a revision; mandatory recording of leaks/missingness/divergence. Confirmatory revisions after observation require a new study or an exploratory label.

## Reporting

All cell outcomes, raw counts and denominators, known missingness, task-family heterogeneity, each training replicate, actual token/latency burden, rhetoric separate from conduct, uncertainty, all deviations, planned versus executed counts, scoped negative findings, and links to allowed evidence artifacts.
